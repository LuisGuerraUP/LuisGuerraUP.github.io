  1. EPJAM
    Optimisation and precision tuning of localised surface plasmon resonance in AuFON systems
    Luis Alfonso Guerra Hernández, Osmar Gil Salas, Jorge Enrique Rueda Parada, and 2 more authors
    EPJ Applied Metamaterials, 2025
    Abs DOI Bib

    This work examines the optimisation of Au-film on nanosphere (AuFON) plasmonic structures using experimental measurements and numerical simulations. The study identifies localised surface plasmon resonances and their near-field spatial distributions, analysing how nanostructure dimensions and illumination conditions influence the optical response. The results provide strategies for enhancing optical signals in molecular detection applications.

  2. RSI
    Green plasmonic mode in AgFON structures: A pathway to enhanced SERS sensitivity
    Luis Alfonso Guerra Hernández, Luis Fernando Tapia Payares, Jorge-Enrique Rueda-Parada, and 3 more authors
    Results in Surfaces and Interfaces, 2025
    Abs DOI Bib

    This work investigates metal-film-on-nanosphere plasmonic structures designed to operate at shorter wavelengths for highly sensitive molecular detection by SERS. A high-energy green plasmonic mode is identified in AgFON structures with sphere diameters of approximately 700–800 nm. Experimental measurements and numerical simulations show that the plasmonic resonances can be tuned through the nanostructure geometry, enabling efficient Raman enhancement and optical sensing in the visible spectral range.

2024

  1. BISTUA
    Efficiency and Spatial Homogeneity of the SERS Signal in Plasmonic Antennas: A Study at the Nano and Micro Scales
    Luis Alfonso Guerra Hernández, Oslen Dilayder Jaimes Suarez, and Jorge-Enrique Rueda-Parada
    BISTUA Revista de la Facultad de Ciencias Básicas, 2024
    Abs DOI Bib

    This study compares the SERS efficiency and spatial homogeneity of four gold plasmonic substrates: rough surfaces, spherical cavities, the commercial Klarite substrate, and Au-film-on-nanosphere (AuFON) structures, using 4-MBA as a probe molecule. AuFON exhibits by far the highest average SERS intensity, with a value of approximately 236, compared with 3.4 for rough gold, 3.0 for Klarite, and 2.8 for spherical cavities. AuFON also shows the lowest signal dispersion at 17%, followed by Klarite at 21%, spherical cavities at 31%, and rough surfaces at 51%. These results demonstrate that an ordered nanostructure morphology can simultaneously enhance SERS intensity and spatial uniformity, positioning AuFON as a promising platform for ultrasensitive molecular detection.

2023

  1. JOSA B
    Does the chemical contribution have a secondary role in SERS?
    Luis Alfonso Guerra Hernández, Andrés A. Reynoso, and Alejandro Fainstein
    Journal of the Optical Society of America B, 2023
    Abs DOI Bib

    This work examines the relative contributions of electromagnetic and chemical enhancement mechanisms in surface-enhanced Raman scattering (SERS). Using 4-mercaptobenzoic acid (4-MBA) as a probe molecule on different plasmonic nanostructures, the study analyzes the role of molecule-metal interactions and charge-transfer processes in the Raman enhancement. The results show that the chemical contribution cannot always be considered merely secondary and that both electromagnetic and chemical mechanisms must be considered when interpreting and optimizing SERS performance.

    Gas sensor using surface plasmon resonance, a simulations-based study
    Edinson Leonardo Gélvez, Jorge Enrique Rueda, and Luis Alfonso Guerra Hernández
    BISTUA Revista de la Facultad de Ciencias Básicas, 2022
    Abs DOI Bib

    This work presents a simulation-based study of a gas sensor using surface plasmon resonance. The figure illustrates the multilayer SPR sensing configuration, the optical excitation of surface plasmons, and the spectral response associated with refractive-index changes in the sensing region. The results highlight the potential of SPR structures for gas detection with high sensitivity.
