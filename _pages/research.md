My research focuses primarily on **plasmonics, surface-enhanced Raman spectroscopy (SERS), nanophotonics, optical spectroscopy, and materials characterization**.

### Plasmonics and SERS

I study light–matter interaction in metallic nanostructures, with particular interest in localized and propagating surface plasmons and their application to molecular detection.

My research includes experimental and numerical studies of plasmonic systems such as:

- Rough gold surfaces
- Gold spherical cavities
- AuFON and AgFON nanostructures
- Gold triangular nanoparticles
- Commercial SERS substrates

### Optical Modeling

I use numerical modeling to study electromagnetic field enhancement, plasmonic resonances, and light coupling mechanisms in metallic nanostructures.

### Photorefractive Materials and Nonlinear Optics

I also have research experience in **dynamic holography, photorefractive materials, nonlinear optics, wave mixing, and optical information processing**.
