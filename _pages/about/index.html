I am a physicist, professor, and researcher at the Universidad de Pamplona, Colombia.

My research focuses on plasmonics, surface-enhanced Raman spectroscopy (SERS), nanophotonics, optical spectroscopy, and materials characterization. I am particularly interested in light–matter interaction in metallic nanostructures, optical sensing, and numerical modeling of plasmonic systems.

I also have research experience in dynamic holography, photorefractive materials, nonlinear optics, and optical information processing.

My academic activities combine research, teaching, student supervision, and scientific dissemination.
